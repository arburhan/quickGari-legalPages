Delete your QuickGari account

For passengers and drivers

Option 1: From the app

Open the QuickGari app, open the Menu, tap Settings, then Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: Request by email

If you cannot use the app, send an email from your account's email address to quickgari@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request. Include the name and phone number of your account. We will delete your account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

When you cannot delete yet

Your account cannot be deleted while you have:

  • a booked trip that is not completed (trip OTP not verified)
  • a payment waiting for admin approval
  • a due service charge (drivers)
  • a driver payout that QuickGari has not paid yet (drivers)

What is deleted

  • Your name, email, phone number, password and profile photo
  • Your driving license, vehicle registration card and tax token photos
  • Your open ride requests, bids, return posts and shared vehicle posts (cancelled)
  • Your push notification tokens

What is kept

Records of past trips and payments (date, route, fare, transaction ID) are kept without your personal details for accounting, fraud prevention and legal reasons. Chat messages you already sent stay visible to the other person as "Deleted User". Deletion from the app happens immediately. See our Privacy Policy.
