Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuickGari ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you use the QuickGari mobile app and related services (the "Service"). QuickGari connects passengers with drivers and vehicle owners for rides, empty return vehicles and shared vehicle trips in Bangladesh.

1. Information we collect

Account information (all users):

  • Name, email address, mobile phone number and gender
  • Profile photo
  • Password (stored only in encrypted, hashed form)

Driver and vehicle information (only if you register a vehicle):

  • Vehicle name, brand, model, seat capacity and vehicle photos
  • Vehicle registration details, registration card photo and tax token photo
  • Driving license photo

Trip and payment information:

  • Pickup and drop locations you type in, trip date and time, fare and bids
  • Payment method, amount and transaction ID (for example bKash). We do not collect or store your bKash PIN, card or bank details.
  • Trip OTP verification status

Communication: chat messages you send to other users inside the app.

Device information: a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you trip and payment notifications, and basic technical data such as IP address and app/device type used for security.

The app does not access your device's GPS location, contacts, camera roll in the background, microphone or SMS.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your email with a one-time code
  • To match passengers with drivers and show trip details to both sides
  • To verify vehicles and drivers before they can offer trips
  • To process and verify payments, service charges and driver payouts
  • To send trip, payment, OTP and account notifications by push notification and email
  • To provide customer support and resolve complaints
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and to keep the Service secure

3. What other users can see

When a trip is booked, the passenger and the driver can see each other's name, profile photo and phone number, and the vehicle details, so they can contact each other for the trip. Vehicle documents (license, registration card and tax token) are only visible to QuickGari administrators.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the Service:

  • Cloudinary – storage of profile, vehicle and document photos
  • Microsoft Azure – server hosting
  • MongoDB Atlas – database hosting
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – push notifications
  • Google Gmail – sending OTP and trip emails

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Data security

Passwords and OTP codes are stored in hashed form. Access to administrative data is restricted to authorized staff. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app (Menu > Settings > Delete Account) or from our Delete Account page.

When you delete your account:

  • Your name, email, phone number, password and profile photo are removed
  • Your driving license, registration card and tax token photos are deleted
  • Your open ride requests, bids, return posts and shared vehicle posts are cancelled
  • Your notification tokens are removed and you are logged out

Records of completed trips and payments (date, route, fare, transaction ID) are kept without your personal details for accounting, fraud prevention and legal requirements. Chat messages you already sent stay visible to the other person, shown as "Deleted User". An account cannot be deleted while it has a booked trip that is not completed, a payment waiting for approval, a due service charge or an unpaid driver payout.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your rights

You can view and update your profile information in the app, and you can ask us for a copy or correction of your data by contacting us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
